under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0-or-later)** and lets
the Maintainer license the project in other ways as well (for example a commercial license
for companies that cannot use the AGPL). It does **not** transfer ownership of your
Contribution: you keep your copyright. The rules below are binding, not just an
introduction.

## 0. Definitions

**"Maintainer"** means Ahmed A. Mansy (GitHub: `AhmadMansy`), and any Successor.

**"Successor"** means a person or legal entity (such as a company operating as Voxarium) to
which the Maintainer assigns or transfers the Maintainer's rights in the project, **but only
if** the Successor accepts in writing the obligations in Section 2 (Open Source Commitment)
and the Maintainer gives public notice of the transfer in the project repository.

**"Project"** means the Voxarium software and its documentation, hosted at
`github.com/AhmadMansy/voxarium` and any successor repository.

**"Contribution"** means any work of authorship, including code, documentation, data and
images, that you intentionally submit to the Project in any form, including pull requests,
patches, issues and comments, **whether made before or after you sign this agreement**.
Work that you conspicuously mark in writing as "Not a Contribution" is excluded.

**"Open License"** means the AGPL-3.0-or-later, or any later license that is approved by the
Open Source Initiative or the Free Software Foundation as a free/open source license.

## 2. Open Source Commitment

The Maintainer and every Successor commit that:

1. The Project's publicly available source code stays available under an Open License.
2. If the Maintainer ever releases a Contribution under non-open terms (for example a
   commercial license), the same version of that Contribution also stays available to the
   public under an Open License.
3. Nothing in Section 1.1 allows the Maintainer to withdraw a version of the Project that
   was already released under an Open License. Those versions remain under that license
   for everyone who received them.

## 5. Public record and personal data **[COUNSEL]**

The Project is public. Your Contribution, your name or username, and the email address in
your commits are public and may be kept in the Project's history indefinitely, because
removing them would break the history that the Open License relies on.

When you sign, the CLA bot records your GitHub username, the date and the version of this
agreement you signed in a public signature file. The Maintainer processes this data to
keep a record of the licenses you granted (performance of this agreement and the
Maintainer's legitimate interest in proving its rights). Where data-protection law
(such as the GDPR) applies, you may ask the Maintainer to correct data about you, and to
erase data that is not needed to prove the licenses you granted. Licenses you already
granted are not undone by such a request. Contact the Maintainer by opening an issue in the
Project's GitHub repository (do not include personal data in a public issue; ask for a private
way to continue and the Maintainer will arrange one).

## 6. Versions and changes

This agreement is versioned. Your signature covers the version you signed. If the Maintainer
publishes a new version, it applies to Contributions you make after you sign that new
version. The Maintainer cannot make a new version reduce the rights that an earlier version
gave you, in particular the Open Source Commitment in Section 2.

2. **Severability.** If a part of this agreement is unenforceable, the rest stays in force,

3. **No waiver.** Not enforcing a right now does not give up that right.
4. **Assignment.** You may not assign this agreement. The Maintainer may assign it only to a

5. **Governing law and courts [COUNSEL].** This agreement is governed by the laws of the

   apply to you where you live are not affected.

## How to sign

Open a pull request. The CLA bot will comment with instructions. Reply with the sentence

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

and the bot records your GitHub username. You only sign once for each version of this
agreement. By signing, you accept this Contributor License Agreement.

**If you contribute on behalf of your employer** or any other organization, the organization
must sign a corporate contributor agreement first (see Section 3.2).
