# Code of Conduct

Voxarium follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the repository, issues, discussions, pull requests and any space where someone
represents the project.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept feedback graciously, and keep
discussion about the work. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing others'
private information, and sexualised language or imagery are not acceptable.

## Voice and likeness

Because this project is about synthetic speech, one extra rule applies: **do not use project
spaces to share or request cloned voices of real people without their consent**, and do not
post impersonation, fraud or deception examples. Such posts will be removed.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately through GitHub: open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/AhmadMansy/voxarium/security/advisories/new)
and mark it as a conduct report, or contact a maintainer directly on GitHub. Reports are kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove content, warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban
participants, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
